Processing order

  1. Audio is downloaded and duration is checked.
  2. Stems are separated and matched to the requested parts.
  3. Known meter, tempo, feel, tuning, and capo settings guide the draft.
  4. Notes are inferred, cleaned, and positioned on the fretboard.
  5. A .gp5 file is written as an editable tab draft.

Heads up

CPU separation can take a while on dense mixes. Fast mode skips stem separation.
