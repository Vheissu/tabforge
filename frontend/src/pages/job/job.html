Processing order

  1. Audio is downloaded and duration is checked.
  2. Stems are separated and analyzed for tempo.
  3. Notes are transcribed and positioned on the fretboard.
  4. A .gp5 file is written and attached to this job.

Heads up

CPU separation can take a while on dense mixes. Fast mode skips stem separation.
